About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was founded in 2017 by Lindiwe Dube, who set out to bring warm, freshly baked bread and pastries to the local community using traditional family recipes.

Our Mission

We believe good food brings people together. Every loaf, cake, and pastry we bake is made with quality ingredients and genuine care, so every visit feels like coming home.

Meet the Team

Our small team of dedicated bakers works before sunrise every day to make sure our shelves are full of fresh bread, croissants, and cakes by the time we open our doors.
